Professional profile

Some problems are too heavy for a person to carry or fix alone. Society responds through social work, and that is the profession I am training for.

But helping people takes time, and too much of it goes to gathering and organizing information before real help can begin. I want to change that.

At work, I already build automations that capture and organize information without manual entry. My goal is to bring that into social work: faster data gathering, and a secure, confidential database that only the right people can see, so social workers spend less time on paperwork and more time with the people who need them.

    Case studies

    Each case follows the STAR method: Situation, Task, Action, Result.

    Work · New Wine Digitals

    Automating lead intake from Facebook forms

    ~1,500contacts a week reached by each of 6 live flows

    1. Situation

      Every new lead from a Facebook (Meta) lead form has to be recorded and contacted. Done by hand, that means typing each lead into the system and waiting for someone to send the first message.

    2. Task

      Build a workflow that records each lead and starts the conversation on its own.

    3. Action

      I set the lead forms as triggers, then had the workflow create or update an opportunity in the pipeline and send the first SMS right away. It waits for a reply: a reply updates the record and notifies a team member, and no reply updates the record so follow-up continues.

    4. Result

      Six workflows like this are live and fully working. Each reaches about 1,500 contacts a week on average, and no lead is typed in by hand.

    Work · New Wine Digitals

    A follow-up sequence that runs for more than a week

    ~200SMS a day, all sent automatically

    1. Situation

      Contacts who do not reply right away still need follow-up over the next several days.

    2. Task

      Keep in touch with each contact without anyone sending messages by hand.

    3. Action

      A tag added by an earlier step starts the sequence. After a 1-hour wait, contacts split into an email path and an SMS path. Follow-ups go out after waits of a day or more, splitting again at each stage.

    4. Result

      Each contact gets five follow-ups over more than a week, by email or SMS. Across my workflows, about 200 SMS go out every day with no manual sending.

    Coursework · SW 210

    A guided interview tool for a client experiencing depression

    77questions in 10 sections, in English and Bisaya

    1. Situation

      For my SW 210 casework, my assigned client category was “mental health problem,” and I needed a tool to guide an interview with a client experiencing depression.

    2. Task

      Build a guided questionnaire that follows the class format for a Case Assessment Report and looks at the whole person: biological, psychological, social and environmental.

    3. Action

      I wrote the questions with Bisaya translations, added an opening that asks for consent and explains the limits of confidentiality, included the NIMH Ask Suicide-Screening Questions (ASQ) word for word, and listed verified crisis hotline numbers.

    4. Result

      A 7-page interview tool with 77 questions in 10 sections, ready to use in two languages. It holds questions only, so no client data is stored in it.
